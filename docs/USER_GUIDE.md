# Freelancian MVP - User Guide

## Welcome to Freelancian

Freelancian is a financial tracking application designed specifically for Thai freelancers. This guide will help you get started with tracking your income and expenses efficiently.

## Getting Started

### Dashboard Overview

When you first open Freelancian, you'll see the dashboard with:
- **Current Month Metrics**: Total income, expenses, and net amount
- **Recent Entries**: Your last 10 financial entries
- **Quick Actions**: Add new entry and view reports buttons

### Key Features

## 1. Manual Entry Creation

### Creating Income Entries

1. Click **"Add Entry"** from the dashboard or navigate to **"New Entry"**
2. Select **"Income"** as the entry type
3. Fill in the required information:
   - **Title**: Description of the income (e.g., "Voice Over Project")
   - **Client Name**: Name of the client paying you
   - **Product/Service**: What you provided
   - **Gross Amount**: The base amount before taxes
   - **Date**: When the work was completed or payment received

4. **Auto-calculation helpers**:
   - Toggle **"Auto VAT (7%)"** to automatically calculate VAT
   - Toggle **"Auto WHT (3%)"** to automatically calculate withholding tax
   - The system will show you the final net amount in real-time

5. Click **"Save Entry"** to create the record

### Creating Expense Entries

1. Follow the same steps but select **"Expense"** as the entry type
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: What you purchased (e.g., "Office Supplies")
   - **Vendor Name**: Where you bought it from
   - **Amount**: How much you spent
   - **Date**: When the expense occurred

### Live Preview Feature

As you type, the right side of the screen shows a live preview of how your entry will look. This helps you verify all information is correct before saving.

## 2. Managing Your Entries

### Viewing All Entries

1. Navigate to **"Entries"** from the main menu
2. You'll see all your entries with:
   - Entry type (Income/Expense)
   - Title and amount
   - Date and client/vendor information
   - Quick action buttons

### Searching and Filtering

- **Search**: Type in the search box to find entries by title, client, or vendor name
- **Filter by Type**: Choose "Income" or "Expense" to see only those entries
- **Filter by Month**: Select a specific month to view entries from that period
- **Filter by Client/Vendor**: Choose from your existing clients or vendors

### Editing Entries

1. Click the **edit icon** on any entry card
2. A slide-out panel will appear with the entry details
3. Make your changes and click **"Update Entry"**

### Deleting Entries

1. Click the **delete icon** on any entry card
2. Confirm the deletion in the popup dialog
3. The entry will be permanently removed

## 3. Financial Reports

### Dashboard Metrics

The dashboard shows your current month's financial summary:
- **Total Income**: All income entries for the current month
- **Total Expenses**: All expense entries for the current month
- **Net Amount**: Income minus expenses (your profit/loss)

### Detailed Reports

1. Click **"View Reports"** from the dashboard
2. You'll see:
   - **Monthly Chart**: Visual representation of income vs expenses over time
   - **Summary Statistics**: Detailed breakdown with trend indicators
   - **Period Selector**: Choose different time periods to analyze

### Understanding Trends

- **Green arrows** indicate positive growth compared to the previous period
- **Red arrows** indicate decline compared to the previous period
- **Percentage changes** show how much your finances have changed

## 4. Data Import (CSV)

### Importing from Notion or Excel

If you have existing financial data in CSV format:

1. Navigate to **"Import"** from the main menu
2. Choose whether you're importing **Income** or **Expense** data
3. Click **"Choose File"** and select your CSV file
4. The system will preview your data and suggest field mappings
5. Verify the mappings are correct and click **"Import Data"**
6. Review the import summary for any errors or skipped entries

### CSV Format Requirements

Your CSV should include columns for:
- Title/Description
- Amount
- Date
- Client/Vendor name (optional)
- Additional details (optional)

## 5. Thai Tax Calculations

### Understanding VAT (Value Added Tax)

- Standard VAT rate in Thailand is **7%**
- For income entries, VAT is added to your gross amount
- Use the auto-calculation toggle for convenience

### Understanding WHT (Withholding Tax)

- Standard withholding tax rate is **3%**
- This is tax withheld by clients and paid directly to the government
- Reduces your net income but you can claim it back during tax filing
- The system warns you if withholding exceeds 3% of gross amount

### Net Amount Calculation

**For Income:**
Net Amount = Gross Amount + VAT - Withholding Tax - Commission

**For Expenses:**
Net Amount = Gross Amount + VAT - Withholding Tax

## Tips for Success

### Best Practices

1. **Enter data regularly**: Don't wait until month-end to input your entries
2. **Use consistent naming**: Keep client and vendor names consistent for better reporting
3. **Include details**: Add project names and remarks for better tracking
4. **Review monthly**: Check your reports monthly to understand your financial trends

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Mixing currencies**: The system is designed for Thai Baht only
2. **Incorrect tax calculations**: Use the auto-calculation features to avoid errors
3. **Missing dates**: Always include accurate dates for proper reporting
4. **Duplicate entries**: Check existing entries before creating new ones

## Getting Help

### Common Issues

**Q: My calculations don't look right**
A: Double-check that you're using the correct tax rates (7% VAT, 3% WHT) and that your gross amounts are accurate.

**Q: I can't find an entry I created**
A: Use the search function or check your filters - you might be viewing only income when looking for an expense entry.

**Q: The import failed**
A: Check that your CSV file has the correct format and that dates are in a recognizable format (YYYY-MM-DD works best).

**Q: The app is slow on my phone**
A: Try closing other browser tabs and ensure you have a stable internet connection.

### Support

For technical issues or feature requests, please check the application's error messages which provide specific guidance for resolving common problems.
